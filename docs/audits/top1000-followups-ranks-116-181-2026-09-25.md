Registry terms were checked before considering fixture retention. The `.md`
server points to its [registration rules](https://nic.md/img/legal/Rules_registration_subdomain_md.pdf),
which describe a public, domain-name-keyed WHOIS lookup but do not grant a
redistribution right. The `.lu` [WHOIS policy](https://dns.lu/files/documents/2025-05/RegistreLu_en_WhoisPolicy.pdf)
limits use to listed registration/verification and technical purposes and
restricts other use of its database. The `.nu` registry says its WHOIS data is
protected by Swedish copyright/database rights and restricts commercial and
bulk use ([registry search rules](https://internetstiftelsen.se/en/domains/how-to-register-a-domain-name/rules-and-description-of-domain-name-searches/)).
For `.host`, the [Radix WHOIS access policy](https://radix.website/policies)
allows query-based access and prohibits bulk/data-mining uses. For `.ac`, the
[Identity Digital RDDS policy](https://www.identity.digital/policies/rdds-access-policy)
prohibits mass solicitation and high-volume automated querying. No bulk
collection or prohibited purpose was attempted. Regardless of those
distinctions, exact bytes were not persisted, so no fresh fixtures were
eligible to commit. No customer data was used.
