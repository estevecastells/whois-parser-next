## Scope and ordering

This range follows the ICANN DNS Magnitude snapshot for 12 September 2026,
generated 18 September 2026, from the [official snapshot page](https://magnitude.research.icann.org/).
The ordering is the source table's `Daily Rank` sequence, with only `.arpa`
excluded, matching the Top 100 audit contract. Special-use and rows without a
delegation status remain in their source positions. This is important for
joining this range to ranks 126-150 without silently shifting or duplicating
entries.

These are parser and source-support results, not availability guarantees. A
restricted, unavailable, unsupported, refused, ambiguous, or unresolved
outcome must remain distinguishable from an authoritative available or absent
result.

## Safety contract

The typed unsupported-registry parsers recognize only the observed explicit
`TLD is not supported.` or `No Data Found` denial shape and raise
`Whois::ResponseIsUnavailable` before exposing status, availability, or
registration properties. Standard mappings without a dedicated parser remain
unsupported rather than guessing. Unknown responses in the `.ke`, `.hr`,
`.one`, and `.ly` paths do not become registered by fallback.

Fixtures under
`spec/fixtures/responses/audit_20260919_ranks101_125/` are sanitized extracts
from the live port-43 responses collected during this audit. They retain the
status and absence markers used by the parser tests while removing personal
contact details and volatile response footers. Rows without port-43 support
are represented by their explicit web-only, no-adapter, timeout, or denial
classification instead of being treated as availability evidence.

The dedicated range spec passed 23 examples. The relevant existing parser
response suites passed 128 examples with 0 failures.

## Follow-up evidence, 2026-09-25

IANA currently lists `whois.cctld.uz` and `whois.nic.net.sa` as the official
port-43 servers for `.uz` and `.sa`; the existing `whois` server mappings
resolve to those same hosts. On 2026-09-25, bounded direct queries to those
hosts returned the sanitized evidence below:

```text
whois.cctld.uz  | google.uz
Domain Name: GOOGLE.UZ
Status: ACTIVE

whois.cctld.uz  | codex-audit-20260925-uz104.uz
Sorry, but domain: "codex-audit-20260925-uz104.uz", not found in database

whois.nic.net.sa | saudigazette.com.sa
Domain Name: SAUDIGAZETTE.COM.SA

whois.nic.net.sa | codex-audit-20260925-sa105.com.sa
No Match for domain: codex-audit-20260925-sa105.com.sa
```

The `.uz` parser now requires a valid echoed `.uz` `Domain Name` plus a single
exact `Status: ACTIVE` or `Status: RESERVED` line for those outcomes. It treats
the exact, query-echoing `Sorry, but domain: "…uz", not found in database`
line as registry absence; other or conflicting statuses stay unknown. A
reserved `.uz` response remains `:reserved`, with `registered?` and
`available?` both false.

The `.sa` parser recognizes registered `Domain Name: …sa` lines and exact
`No Match for …sa` lines, including the current `No Match for domain:` form
and the older fixture form. Both are full-line/suffix anchored; conflicting,
off-suffix, and unrecognized lines remain unknown. Empty, denied, and
rate-limited responses are exercised with record-looking lines to ensure
response safety wins over apparent status evidence.

The optional rank-122 `.ge` check used IANA's current delegation record, which
lists `whois.nic.ge`. Bounded direct port-43 queries to that host returned:

```text
whois.nic.ge | google.ge
Domain Name: GOOGLE.GE
Domain Status: Ok

whois.nic.ge | codex-audit-20260925-ge122.ge
No match for "CODEX-AUDIT-20260925-GE122.GE".
```

The new `WhoisNicGe` parser recognizes only the observed full-line `.ge`
registered marker plus `Domain Status: Ok`, or the exact quoted `.ge`
absence marker. Other statuses, conflicts, off-suffix lines, empty responses,
denials, and rate limits do not become availability or registration results.
The installed `whois` 6.0.3 runtime still routes `.ge` to
`whois.registration.ge`; this change does not alter default server routing.
DomScan must select IANA's `whois.nic.ge` through its bounded fixed-host
standard WHOIS path before counting this parser as effective coverage.

Probe budgets were a 4-second connection timeout, 7-second total response
deadline, 64 KiB response cap, and at least 2 seconds between queries. Only
four primary probes were issued, plus one repeat of the short `.sa` absence
response to inspect its exact marker. The test fixtures are sanitized to the
queried name and classification lines; no registrant/contact fields are
retained. These WHOIS absence results are not proof of registration
claimability.
