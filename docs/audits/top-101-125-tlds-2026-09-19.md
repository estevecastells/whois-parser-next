## Scope and ordering

This range follows the ICANN DNS Magnitude snapshot for 12 September 2026,
generated 18 September 2026, from the [official snapshot page](https://magnitude.research.icann.org/).
The ordering is the source table's `Daily Rank` sequence, with only `.arpa`
excluded, matching the Top 100 audit contract. Special-use and rows without a
delegation status remain in their source positions. This is important for
joining this range to ranks 126-150 without silently shifting or duplicating
entries.

These are parser and source-support results, not availability guarantees. A
restricted, unavailable, unsupported, refused, ambiguous, or unresolved
outcome must remain distinguishable from an authoritative available or absent
result.

## Safety contract

The typed unsupported-registry parsers recognize only the observed explicit
`TLD is not supported.` or `No Data Found` denial shape and raise
`Whois::ResponseIsUnavailable` before exposing status, availability, or
registration properties. Standard mappings without a dedicated parser remain
unsupported rather than guessing. Unknown responses in the `.ke`, `.hr`,
`.one`, and `.ly` paths do not become registered by fallback.

Fixtures under
`spec/fixtures/responses/audit_20260919_ranks101_125/` are sanitized extracts
from the live port-43 responses collected during this audit. They retain the
status and absence markers used by the parser tests while removing personal
contact details and volatile response footers. Rows without port-43 support
are represented by their explicit web-only, no-adapter, timeout, or denial
classification instead of being treated as availability evidence.

The dedicated range spec passed 23 examples. The relevant existing parser
response suites passed 128 examples with 0 failures.
