# WHOIS parser DNS Magnitude ranks 1-100 audit

Date: 2026-09-19

## Scope and ordering

This document records the after-fix status from the integrated live audit. The
table preserves the exact `Daily Rank` and TLD sequence from the [ICANN DNS
Magnitude snapshot](https://magnitude.research.icann.org/) dated 2026-09-12,
generated 2026-09-18. Source ranks are retained, including special-use,
undelegated, and infrastructure rows. No rows are removed or renumbered for
parser-scope purposes. The later audit ranges continue at source rank 101.

The integrated audit now covers ranks 1-1000. The combined parser and audit
suite passes 6,768 examples with 0 failures.

These are parser and source-support results, not availability guarantees. A
restricted, unavailable, unsupported, refused, ambiguous, or unresolved
outcome must remain distinguishable from an authoritative available or absent
result.

## Interpretation

`Fixed` identifies a registry or server-family behavior addressed in this
audit. `Healthy` identifies a path that passed without a new parser fix.
Unsupported, restricted, refused, transient, ambiguous, unresolved, non-public,
special-use, undelegated, infrastructure, and web-only results describe the
current evidence or access path; they do not assert domain availability.

The audit is a point-in-time review of live WHOIS behavior and parser coverage.
Registry formats, access policies, rate limits, and endpoint availability can
change after the observation date. Source ranks 101-125 are documented in the
[next range report](top-101-125-tlds-2026-09-19.md) and do not overlap this
table.
