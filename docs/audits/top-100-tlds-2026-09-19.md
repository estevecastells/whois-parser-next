## Interpretation

`Fixed` identifies a registry or server-family behavior addressed in this
audit. `Healthy` identifies a path that passed without a new parser fix.
`Unsupported`, restricted, refused, transient, ambiguous, unresolved,
non-public, and web-only results describe the current evidence or access path;
they do not assert domain availability.

The audit is a point-in-time review of live WHOIS behavior and parser coverage.
Registry formats, access policies, rate limits, and endpoint availability can
change after the observation date.
